    The Fountains' Hidden Engineering

    Thomas Moreau presents new research on how the 17th-century network of reservoirs and gravity-fed pipes still feeds the Versailles fountains today, and on the modern pumps that keep it running. Free and open to the public; no reservation required.

    When
    18:30–20:00
    Where
    Versailles Vivant, 14 Rue de la Paroisse, Versailles
    No reservation needed
