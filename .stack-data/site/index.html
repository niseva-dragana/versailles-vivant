Who we are

Not the Château. Not a tour company either.

Versailles Vivant is an independent nonprofit — not an official arm of the Château de Versailles, and not a booking agency. We're some 640 members who care about the place enough to fund garden apprentices' stipends, print a proper history pamphlet, or spend a Saturday weeding a parterre most visitors never look at twice. Membership dues and public donations cover almost all of it.

Where the money went in 2025

€145,000 in all, almost entirely from membership dues and donations.

Explore the estate

Six places worth a closer look.

See all six
Entry 01

The Hall of Mirrors

Seventeen arcades of mirror glass — 357 mirrors in all — face the garden windows across a hall 73 metres long. About 70% of the mirrors are still original.

Entry 02

The Royal Apartments

In 1701 Louis XIV moved his bedchamber to the centre of the east façade, facing the rising sun. Court etiquette fixed exactly who could watch the lever du roi.

Entry 03

Gardens & Fountains

An estate of about 800 hectares. Le Nôtre began reshaping the gardens in 1661, and Louis XIV later wrote his own guide to walking them.
