Virtual Tour

Walk through, from wherever you are.

Five real spaces on the estate, in sequence. Use the arrows, the numbers below the photo, the arrow keys on your keyboard, or swipe on a touch screen.

Photo: Unsplash

Stop 1 of 5

Gardens & Fountains

André Le Nôtre's geometry, laid out to be read from the palace windows above. Somewhere behind you is where the fountain crew used to sprint ahead of the king's route, switching jets on just in time.

Stop 2 of 5

The Hall of Mirrors

Look up before you look at the mirrors — the painted ceiling tells the political story the whole room was built to advertise. The mirrors were the expensive part; the ceiling was the point.

Stop 3 of 5

Royal Apartments

Every object in these rooms was chosen to be watched being used. Privacy, in the modern sense, wasn't really part of the design brief — being seen was the entire function of the space.

Stop 4 of 5

Statue Gardens

Marble figures scattered through the grounds, most modelled on classical myths a 17th-century visitor would have recognised instantly and most modern visitors walk straight past without a glance.

Stop 5 of 5

Above the Parterres

The view the garden was designed for, and one most visitors never get: the geometry only fully resolves from above, which is why any upper-floor window is worth finding.

Arrow keys and swiping work too.
