About

A small association with a specific job.

Not the Château's marketing arm, not a heritage charity with a national remit — just over six hundred people who decided the gaps were worth filling.

Photo: Unsplash

How it started

One stalled hedge-restoration project, in 2016.

Versailles Vivant started in 2016, when a handful of local residents — a retired conservator, two garden enthusiasts and a history teacher — noticed that the restoration of a badly damaged stretch of hedge had stalled for lack of funding. They organised a small fundraiser. It covered about a third of what the work needed. The association exists because that felt like a start worth continuing, not a failure to give up on.

Milestones

  1. 2016 Founded, after a hedge restoration stalled for lack of money.
  2. 2019 270 members, after three years of fast growth.
  3. 2020 Marie Laurent becomes president of the board.
  4. 2023 Our first garden apprentice starts her training.
  5. 2025 620 members, 24 events and €145,000 spent.
  6. 2026 Our first apprentice joins the estate's own gardening team.
What we do

Three things, mostly.

01

Garden stewardship

Funding stipends for three garden apprentices at a time, and covering maintenance costs for sections of the gardens that don't generate ticket revenue on their own.

02

Public education

Free public talks, a quarterly journal, and an online archive built from research our own volunteers have put together over the years.

03

Access & community

Members-only mornings, family-friendly Garden Saturdays and a volunteer programme open to anyone — no application form, just come along.

How we're run

A volunteer board, elected by the members.

Seven unpaid board seats, elected at the general assembly each March. Day-to-day programming and the apprenticeship fund are run by two part-time staff, the only paid roles in the association. The annual report and the full accounts are published every spring, garden-maintenance bill included.

The association

The formal details.

  • Legal form: a non-profit association under the French law of 1 July 1901 (association loi 1901), open to anyone who pays the membership fee.
  • Registration: declared at the Préfecture des Yvelines, RNA number W000000000 (a placeholder).
  • General assembly: once a year, in March. Members approve the accounts and elect the board; the last one was held on 14 March 2026.
  • Statutes and annual report: the statutes (PDF, French original with an English translation) and the 2025 annual report (PDF, in English). The main figures are also on the Impact page.
  • Seat: 14 Rue de la Paroisse, 78000 Versailles — a real street, used here as a fictional address.
