Get Involved

Membership, volunteering, or just a donation.

There's no single “right” way to support us. Pick whichever of the three fits your life.

Membership

Three tiers, all month-to-month.

Individual

€9 / month

Three members-only mornings a year before the gates open, the quarterly print journal, and full access to the online archive.

Join as an individual
Family

€16 / month

Everything in Individual, for up to five people at one address, with children welcome on Garden Saturdays.

Join as a family
Patron

€40 / month

Everything in Individual, your name (or anonymity, if you'd rather) in the annual report, and an invitation to the yearly patrons' dinner in the Orangerie.

Volunteer your time

No membership required for any of these.

Hands-on

Garden Saturdays

One Saturday a month, weeding and maintaining the sections of parterre that don't make it into the brochure. No experience needed; gloves provided.

Behind the scenes

Archive & Research

Helping catalogue historical documents and photographs for our journal and public talks. Useful if you're the type who reads footnotes for fun.

Public-facing

Programme Support

Helping run our public talks and members' mornings: setting up, the welcome desk and answering visitors' questions.

Prefer to just give

One-time support works too.

A single donation of any size goes into the same fund as membership dues — mostly garden upkeep and apprenticeships, and keeping the public talks free. €180 covers about a week of one apprentice's stipend; anything less still helps.

Make a one-time gift

How giving would work

In a real association you could give by bank transfer or cheque, and we would send you a tax receipt: in France, gifts to associations of general interest reduce income tax by 66% of the amount given, up to 20% of taxable income. This site takes no payments and issues no receipts: the form below only records your offer.
